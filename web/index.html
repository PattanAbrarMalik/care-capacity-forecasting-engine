02 / FEATURE ENGINEERING

PROJECT-DEFINED INDICATORS

Transfers and discharges

Observed activity on each reporting date

HHS flow balance

Transfers minus discharges · zero is numerical balance

Load-change volatility

14-observation standard deviation of changes / mean load × 100

Pressure rule sensitivity

Positive net-flow sum plus the required positive observations

Full-dataset comparison. Thresholds are analytical conventions, not official operational limits.

What these indicators contribute

Persistence distinguishes a sustained flow pattern from an isolated observation. Sensitivity analysis reveals how much the classification changes when the rule changes. Flow timing and missing reporting dates prevent this proxy from being interpreted as the exact change in census.

04 / DATA UNDERSTANDING

Audit before interpretation

SOURCE TRACEABILITY

Reporting coverage by month

Observed dates / calendar days in each month · first and last months may be partial

Cleaning decisions

Only fully blank source rows are removed. Counts with thousands separators are parsed as integers. Invalid dates, duplicate dates, missing counts, and negative counts fail validation.

Review flags and analytical boundaries

Transfers may exceed the end-of-day custody snapshot because children move through custody within the day. Flagged records remain in the analysis. This file contains no beds, staffing, care acuity, or operational capacity denominator.

Rank correlations are descriptive associations. A common time trend can produce a strong relationship without proving causality.

Metric dictionary

Total load
CBP custody + HHS care on a reported date.
Net flow proxy
CBP transfers out − HHS discharges.
Offset ratio
Sum of discharges / sum of transfers in a stated window.
Pressure flag
Positive sum over 7 observations with at least 4 positive observations.
Volatility
100 × standard deviation of 14 load changes / 14-observation mean load.
MAE
Mean absolute forecast error in children; lower is better.
RMSE
Root mean squared error, which weights larger misses more heavily.
